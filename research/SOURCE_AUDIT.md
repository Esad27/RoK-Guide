## Important corrections to the seed data

- Codex Helper is primarily a Discord bot/toolkit, not simply a public web calculator suite. Some
  commander/meta/planning features are explicitly Patreon features.
- MetaRoK exposes separate open-field, rally, garrison, swarming and investment views. Its rankings
  are strategic/meta judgments.
- Rise of Kingdoms Guides has wider coverage than the original description, including calculators,
  progression, troop, map, event and update material. Recency labels alone still need scrutiny.
- An official category was missing. The official game site and Lilith community area are now seeded.
- The RoK Nut Academy and the specific Fandom Commander Guide were not fully readable through the
  research fetcher; no factual claim should cite inaccessible content as if it were inspected.
