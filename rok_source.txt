STRICT VIDEO RULE
==================================================

Do not automatically select videos based solely on views.

For each video calculate suitability conceptually using:

- Recency
- Language
- Topic relevance
- Creator credibility
- Educational depth
- Compatibility with the site's commander/KvK stage

For commander meta/investment content:

Prefer videos from 2025-2026.

For stable beginner mechanics:

2024 videos may still be acceptable.

Never show publication date, views or subscriber count unless that
metadata has been explicitly stored and verified.

YouTube view counts are research signals only and should not be hardcoded
into the production website.
