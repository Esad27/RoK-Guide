Hospital risk depends on **where and how the battle occurs**. Do not apply one casualty rule to open field, city attacks, rallies and structure defense.

## Understand the wound context

- In ordinary map/open-field combat, severely wounded troops are normally sent to your Hospitals.
- When reinforcing an allied city or allied structure, part of the severe wounds can become dead troops.
- When attacking player cities or alliance buildings, severe wounds can become dead troops instead of entering Hospitals.
- If Hospital capacity is already full, additional severe wounds that cannot enter are lost.

Special events and season rules can differ. Check the battle report and the current in-game description before committing to a high-risk rally or garrison.

## Prepare capacity and resources

Upgrade Hospitals and applicable technology before the fighting window. Capacity only stores severe wounds; healing still consumes resources and time. Estimate the cost for your troop tier and keep the required resource mix available.

## Heal around Alliance Helps

Alliance members do not send a generic healing-speed buff. They can press **Help**, reducing the active healing timer according to the alliance system. During active war, many players use manageable healing batches so completed Helps free troops sooner; the best batch size depends on your Help count, technology and available attention.

## Field discipline

- Check capacity before leaving the city and during long fights.
- Retreat before a losing march becomes a capacity problem.
- Do not fill a rally or garrison until you understand the death rules for that target.
- Keep healing resources in reserve and avoid beginning a long batch just before an urgent fight unless your alliance plan allows it.

Hospital capacity is a limit to manage, not a promise that every casualty in every combat mode is recoverable.
