Open field combat during KvK is higher stakes than regular kingdom play: losses are harder to
recover from mid-season, and the enemy alliance is actively trying to catch stragglers.

## Before Committing

- Confirm your commander pairing is appropriate for the troop type you're fielding — see
  [Commander Pairings](/pairings) for the F2P and recommended combinations tracked on this site.
- Check your healing capacity. An open field loss you can't heal from is far worse than one you can.
- Move with your alliance's designated group rather than solo — open field is where isolated
  marches get punished hardest during KvK.

## During the Fight

Target selection and timing matter alongside troop count, technology, equipment, buffs and march
health. Retreat before the march is surrounded or Hospital capacity becomes unsafe.

## After the Fight

Report back to your alliance on what worked and what didn't. KvK seasons are long; the information
from one fight should feed into how your alliance approaches the next one.

The linked Vietnamese video is explicitly a **KvK3** composition example, not a universal five-march
prescription. Its automatic caption track exists, but a full transcript was unavailable for review.
