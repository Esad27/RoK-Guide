KvK means **Kingdom versus Kingdom** and is played through the Lost Kingdom. Exact objectives, maps and unlock timing vary by season, so confirm the calendar and rules shown inside your kingdom before committing resources.

## Set a realistic combat role

Decide whether you will field-fight, fill rallies and garrisons, gather, or support objectives. F2P and low-spend accounts usually gain more from one properly skilled and equipped march than from several unfinished marches. Rally and garrison leadership should stay with the account selected by kingdom leadership for that target and season.

## Account checklist

- Finish the City Hall, Academy, Hospital and troop-building upgrades needed for your planned troop tier.
- Research the combat statistics that apply to your chosen troop type; do not spread scarce speedups across every branch without a plan.
- Lock commander skill targets before spending sculptures. Complete usable equipment sets rather than leaving several half-built sets.
- Save action-point items for relevant PvE and honor tasks, and keep resource tokens unopened until the resources are needed.
- Keep enough training and healing speedups for the activity your alliance expects from you.

## Coordinate before entry

Join an alliance with published territory, pass and objective calls. Record the expected fighting window, teleport location and retreat point. Ask which rallies or garrisons you may fill and which troop types are accepted; an individually strong march can still be wasteful when it ignores the kingdom plan.

## Spending context

Do not turn a general checklist into a fixed gem-spending rule. Event timing, VIP level, account age and budget change the answer. Large spenders can build several specialized marches; F2P players should protect universal sculptures and prioritize a march that is usable now.
