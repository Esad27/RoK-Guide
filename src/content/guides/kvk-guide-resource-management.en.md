KvK resources have three main jobs: healing, training and account progression. A useful stockpile is not just a large number; it is the right mix of food, wood, stone and gold available when your combat role needs it.

## Keep flexibility

- Leave resource items unopened until you are ready to spend them. Resources already added to the city balance can be exposed if the city is successfully attacked.
- Estimate healing costs for your troop tier before a major fight and reserve that amount instead of committing everything to a building or technology upgrade.
- Keep training and healing speedups separate in your plan; having resources without time, or time without resources, produces the same stoppage.

## Gather safely and deliberately

Use gathering commanders and match their bonuses to the resource you need. Prefer alliance-territory nodes when the map and alliance plan make them safe. Recall gatherers before a contested area becomes active and do not send combat marches to gather when they are required for an objective.

## Move resources with the real system

The Trading Post sends resources to alliance members subject to its transport capacity and tax rate. Farm accounts can support a main account through this system, but they require time and safe logistics; they are not a substitute for budgeting. Follow your kingdom's rules for farm accounts and resource transfers.

## Common mistakes

- Opening every resource token at once.
- Training continuously and discovering there is nothing left to heal with.
- Gathering in a hot zone while offline.
- Sending resources without accounting for Trading Post tax.
- Ignoring the alliance's scheduled fight window when planning long upgrades.
