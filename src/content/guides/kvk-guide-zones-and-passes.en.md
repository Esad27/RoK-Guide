KvK maps are divided into zones connected by passes. Understanding how they function — in general,
not for any specific kingdom's current map — helps you plan marches and defense before you're
already under pressure.

## What Zones Are For

Zones contain different resources and objectives according to the active Lost Kingdom map. Use the
season's in-game map and Chronicles rather than assuming one fixed zone numbering scheme.

## Passes Control Movement

Passes connect zones and begin with protection periods. An alliance normally needs territory
touching a pass to contest or occupy it. After first occupation, an empty pass can be occupied;
otherwise its reinforced defenders must be defeated. Details can vary by map and event state.

## Planning Around Passes

- Scout before committing a rally through a pass — a contested pass can turn a march into a bad
  trade.
- Keep march speed and rally timers in mind: a slower alliance can still win a pass fight by
  arriving with better timing and correct commander pairings.
- Coordinate with alliance leadership before moving zones; a single rogue march can compromise a
  pass your whole alliance is relying on.

This guide intentionally avoids naming a specific kingdom's current zone layout — that changes
every season and per kingdom, so treat this as the general logic behind zone/pass play rather than
a live map guide.
