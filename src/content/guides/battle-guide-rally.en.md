A rally combines troops from multiple players into a single march under one rally captain. It's the
main way an alliance projects force beyond what any single player could field alone.

## Choosing a Rally Captain

Only the captain's commanders and skills apply to the whole rally, so the captain should have the
strongest verified pairing for the rally's target — not simply the highest personal power.

## Joining a Rally

Joiners contribute troops and power but not commander skills. When joining, send the troop type and
tier the captain has requested rather than whatever is fastest to send — a mismatched troop type can
weaken the whole rally's effectiveness.

## Timing

Rallies take time to fill and march. Confirm the rally's fill time and travel time before
committing, especially if the target could reinforce or move before the rally lands.

## After the Rally

Report results back to your alliance's leadership so future rally targets and captain choices can
be adjusted based on what actually happened.

## Target and withdrawal caveats

City, alliance-structure, pass and PvE rallies do not share the same casualty profile. Confirm the
target rules before joining. Once troops have entered the rallying city, joiners cannot use the
normal recall action; before launch, the captain can remove them. Do not join a high-risk rally
until its captain, troop request and target are clear.
